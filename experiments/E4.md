# E4-A — Verify-then-Trust (VtT): a probabilistic test-time hint sanitiser (method evaluation)

**Status: FROZEN at tag `e4-prereg` (2026-11-09), before any E4 eval run.** Changed afterwards only by dated amendments (§8).

User decision (2026-11-08): go to E4. The method must be algorithmic, not heuristic, and work with the base models; E4-B (a learned trust read-out) may follow; log everything.

## 0. Why a new method (lessons from E3, `experiments/E3.md`, `reports/e2x_results/`)
- E3 judged hints against the model's own photos-only prediction, using thresholds (z · σ). On whole-room clips that witness is wrong, and its uncertainty does not know it.
  - The primary calibration failed.
  - The E3 eval showed CI-excluding harm on correct and real phone hints in rooms.
- Post hoc, E3's false actions concentrate where few photo pairs verify each other (`results/e3_eval_agg/posthoc_support_vs_false_actions.csv`).
- VtT replaces the witness and the thresholds with an explicit probabilistic model:
  - **Evidence:** multi-view epipolar geometry, model-free.
  - **Evidence strength:** the first-order covariance of each two-view estimate.
  - **Evidence reliability:** an explicit prior that an image pair's geometry is simply wrong (wrong matches are common indoors).
  - **Hint reliability:** the same kind of prior for each hint.
  - **Decisions:** posterior quantities. Where the photos cannot verify a hint, the posterior is the prior and the hint is returned unchanged.

## 1. The method (`exp1/vtt.py`; constants in §1.3; frozen at the tag)
**Pipeline per clip and hint set:**
1. **Matches:** `exp1/photo_check.match_pairs` (SIFT, ratio 0.75, RANSAC fundamental matrix at 1.5 px; unchanged since E1). VtT then keeps one-to-one correspondences (`vtt.one_to_one`): exact repeats count once, and keypoints matched to several partners are dropped.
2. **Two-view geometry per pair** (`vtt.pair_edges`):
   - The essential matrix in the hinted intrinsics (RANSAC, 1.5 px) is refined on all inliers by robust Gauss–Newton on the Sampson error (Hartley & Zisserman §11.4.3).
   - Its 5×5 first-order covariance gives Sr (relative rotation) and St (translation direction).
   - The residual scale is floored at 0.1 px (SIFT localisation precision).
   - Pairs with < 15 inliers are not used.
   - **Ambiguity check against the hints** (`vtt.resolve_with_hints`). Short-baseline video (rotation–translation ambiguity) and near-planar views can fit more than one relative pose, and the refinement may settle in the wrong one; its local covariance cannot show this.
     - Each pair's refinement is restarted from the hinted relative pose.
     - If that restart reaches another mode whose robust Sampson cost is statistically equivalent, i.e. 2·(cost_hint − cost_photo)/κ_rot² < χ²₃(1 − α), the pair enters the fusion at the hint-consistent mode (the matches cannot tell the two apart; the prior breaks the tie).
     - Where the matches contradict the hint, the restart returns to the photo mode or costs clearly more, and the pair is unchanged.
3. **Model:**
   - **Unknowns:** the camera rotations R_i. The hinted centres are kept; E1 found position errors are mostly absorbed.
   - **Hint mixture:** each hint is right, H_i = R_i Exp(n), n ~ N(0, σ_h² I), with probability π, or wrong (uniform rotation). σ_h and π are declared priors (§1.3).
   - **Edge mixture:** each pair is right with probability pe(n) = sigmoid(pe_a + pe_b log n), or wrong (uniform rotation and direction). A right pair has:
     - rotation residual ~ N(0, κ_rot² Sr + s_rot² I);
     - direction residual ~ N(0, κ_dir² St + s_dir² I + 2σ_c²/|c_i − c_j|² I).

     s_rot and s_dir are a model-error floor: errors that do not shrink with more matches, such as rolling shutter, residual distortion and synchronisation.
4. **Per-clip hyperparameter:** σ_c (centre-hint noise), by empirical Bayes at the photo-only rotations placed in the world by the directions to the hinted centres. Centre noise is independent per camera, so it is what remains once the best global rotation is removed.
5. **Explanations:** EM (responsibilities of hints and pairs, then a Gauss–Newton step on SO(3)) from three starts:
   - the hints;
   - the photo-only rotations placed by the hints;
   - the photo-only rotations placed by the directions, which is what makes an error shared by all hints visible.

   Each start converges to one explanation (which hints and which pairs are wrong). The explanation kept has the highest **Laplace evidence**, E + ½ log det(Hessian). Integrating over the rotations is what stops one confident wrong pair from overruling a hint: one pair against one hint is decided by their prior reliabilities, while pairs that confirm each other outweigh a hint.
6. **Decision, per hint: a leave-one-out posterior-predictive check** inside the chosen explanation.
   - Compute camera i's posterior from everything except its own hint (mode R_−i, covariance Σ_−i), and let r = Log(H_iᵀ R_−i).
   - The hint is inconsistent if rᵀ(σ_h² I + Σ_−i)⁻¹ r > χ²₃(1 − α), with α = 0.01.
   - **Breakdown requirement:** an inconsistent hint is replaced only if the evidence against it survives removing any single photo pair. The exception is a pair a priori more reliable than a hint, pe(n) > π: for one pair against one hint, the posterior odds are just the prior odds.
   - A replaced hint takes its posterior mode, re-estimated with the other hints held fixed. Consistent hints are returned exactly.
   - Nudging a consistent hint gains about its own small noise, but carries the full risk of unmodelled photo bias.
   - A camera without photo evidence has a wide prediction and keeps its hint.
7. **Depth** (only when pose and depth hints are both given; the pose hints fix the units):
   - **Evidence:** matches triangulated with the sanitised rotations and hinted centres give, per depth view, m_v = median log(depth hint / triangulated depth) and its standard error se_v (`vtt.depth_evidence`).
   - **Decision** (`vtt.depth_decide`, the same label logic): the scale shared by the depth hints is right or wrong, and so is each view alone (prior as for hints).
     - Each label's size if wrong ~ N(0, τ²).
     - Evidence noise se_v² + s_depth² (a calibrated model-error floor), plus a log-scale error shared by all views, N(0, σ_ps²). The depth is measured in the pose hints' units, which have their own scale error (odometry scale drift).
     - The most probable label configuration, by exact enumeration.
   - Only depth hints labelled wrong are rescaled, by their posterior mean scale; a view without evidence takes the shared part.
8. The base model is run once on the rewritten hints (control `vtt`).

### 1.1 What VtT does not act on (stated in advance)
- **Centres** (positions), **intrinsics**, and the **metric scale of pose hints**: photos carry no metric scale.
- **Depth hints without pose hints** (e.g. `ai_depth`): there are no units to check them against. They pass through unchanged, so Δ ≡ 0 is expected; `ai_depth` stays in the test list for comparability with E3 and cannot count towards the claim.
- **Small hint errors** that are consistent with the hint noise prior (§1, step 6): left alone by design.

### 1.2 Control `vtt_gate` (MapAnything only; ablation, descriptive)
- The original hints, with MapAnything's per-view pose fusion gate set to the mixture probability w_i (`model_io.set_hint_gate`).
- This tests whether "soft trust" through the model's own dial does as well as correcting the hint.

### 1.4 Development history (dev / cal clips only)

Each change below was made because a dev check exposed a defect. None was made to improve a number on eval data.

Where each number comes from:
- **Kept files:** `results/e4_dev/history/` (iterations 1–3), `results/e4_dev/cycle_consistency_vs_gt.csv`, and the final checks in `results/e4_dev/`.
- **Console output only:** two later iterations were overwritten and their numbers were only seen in the console. They are marked *(console)*.

1. **Unrefined RANSAC essential matrices** are off by 0.4–2.8° even on clean synthetic data (5-point minimal sample, no final refinement; *(console)*) → Sampson Gauss–Newton refinement with covariances.
2. **SIFT many-to-one matches** let an essential matrix fit a pair exactly (ETH3D `dev__facade__c2`, pair 7–9: 32 matches, 7 distinct points in one image; *(console)*) and pulled correct hints by 27° *(console)* → one-to-one filter and 0.1 px residual floor.
3. **A coherent error shared by all hints** was only partly found → third start (photos placed by directions), and σ_c estimated after removing the best global rotation, so centre noise is not mistaken for a heading error.
4. **Whole-room dev/cal clips** (0–6 pairs per clip, about half of them wrong: `results/e4_dev/history/room_cal_edges.csv`): correct hints moved 4.1° on average over the real dev/cal set (`results/e4_dev/history/dev_room_v1/summary.csv`). One confident wrong pair beat one hint under the Cauchy loss and the MAP rule → explicit edge mixture with a calibrated prior, and Laplace evidence for choosing between explanations.
5. **Hint noise fitted inside each explanation** made explanations incomparable and let noisy centres pull correct hints by about 0.5° (unit test `test_noisy_centres_do_not_move_correct_rotations`) → σ_h and π fitted once per clip (later replaced, item 8).
6. **Depth ×1.5 on two views** was only partly corrected when one view had little evidence (`results/e4_dev/history/dev_eth_v2/summary.csv`) → shared + per-view scale model (later replaced, item 9).
7. **κ pooled over ETH3D (DSLR, κ ≈ 2) and TUM (handheld Kinect video, κ ≈ 10)** *(console, first calibration)* was a compromise wrong for both. Errors of video pairs do not shrink with more matches (rolling shutter: about 30°/s × 25 ms ≈ 0.75°) → noise model κ²·S + s²·I, both fitted by maximum likelihood (`results/e4_vtt_cal/`).
8. **The per-clip fit of σ_h used the photo-only rotations as reference.** Where the photo graph is a tree with wrong pairs, that reference is wrong: σ_h was fitted at 10.5° on `cal__sf483632__c2` *(console)*, and the hints lost their weight. Photo cycles exist in few SceneFun3D clips (`results/e4_dev/cycle_consistency_vs_gt.csv`: median 0–3 extra pairs beyond a tree) → σ_h and π are declared priors.
9. **The mixture label as the decision rule was too lenient.** Against a uniform outlier density, a hint 5σ from a precise posterior still counts as right, so a 5° mount error was found but not corrected (unit test `test_eval_style_shared_rotation_is_corrected`).
   - **Posterior mode as the output was too eager:** correct hints were nudged towards biased video pairs (SceneFun3D clips, about 0.5–0.7° median; *(console)*).
   - **Fix for both:** the leave-one-out predictive check (step 6).
   - **Depth:** now uses the same label logic with a calibrated evidence floor, because correct LiDAR depth had been rescaled *(console)*.

10. **Ambiguous video pairs.** With the leave-one-out rule, rooms and E2-E dev clips came back untouched, but 2 SceneFun3D cal clips still had hints replaced by about 35° (`results/e4_dev/history/iter_loo_before_resolution/`).
    - **Diagnosis** (`results/e4_dev/gt_epipolar_check.csv`): pairs with up to 638 matches gave photo estimates 5–25° from ground truth, yet the ground-truth relative pose fits the same matches at 0.14–0.6 px (median Sampson), as well as the photo estimate does (0.06–0.29 px). The ground truth is right; the pairs are ambiguous.
    - **Not the cause:** Torr's GRIC (homography vs essential matrix) does not flag them (`results/e4_dev/gric_probe.csv`).
    - **Fix:** the ambiguity check against the hints (§1 step 2).
    - **Regression test:** `test_ambiguous_video_pairs_do_not_overrule_correct_hints` (runs where the cal clip is present).

11. **One unverified pair overruling two correct hints.**
    - **Case:** `cal__sf468711__c2`, a single pair (24 matches) 6.4° from the ground truth, labelled right inside its explanation; the leave-one-out prediction rested on it alone.
    - **Before:** the hints' mean pairwise rotation error went from 0 to 1.39° (`results/e4_dev/history/iter_resolution/real_devcal/runs.csv`).
    - **Fix:** the breakdown requirement (§1 step 6).
12. **Depth with iPad poses.** Correct LiDAR depth was rescaled towards the iPad trajectory's own scale error: 13.8% of `dev_all` runs had |log scale| worse by more than 0.05 (`results/e4_dev/history/iter_resolution/real_devcal/runs.csv`).
    - **Fix:** the shared pose-scale term σ_ps (§1 step 7).

**Tests:** `tests/test_vtt.py` (synthetic scenes with known geometry, plus one real cal clip) and `tests/test_vtt_runner.py` (the runner with a fake model).

## 4. Tests (`scripts/e4/analyse_e4.py`; per model)

Shared settings: scene means over clips, paired over scenes; 95% percentile-bootstrap CI; t-test p; practical thresholds of E2 (shape 1 pp, rotation 0.25°, real size 5 pp); one Holm family per model over HELP, FIXED and BEST.

- **SAFE (non-inferiority):**
  - Conditions (correct hints): `clean_pose`, `clean_pose_depth2`, `gt_pose`, `gt_all`, `dev_pose`, `dev_all`.
  - Metrics: shape and rotation, plus real size on real clips.
  - Δ = E[vtt] − E[as given]. A test passes if the CI's upper end is below the threshold.
  - VtT is SAFE if every test passes (intersection–union test).
- **HELP:** E3's list of bad-hint conditions; helpful = Holm-clear and Δ ≤ −threshold.
- **FIXED (new):** VtT against each fixed deployable remedy (R0, R2, R3, E3, E3-drop), each applied to all of one data set's SAFE + HELP conditions.
  - Pooled per scene: rotation over the conditions with pose hints; shape over all.
  - better = Holm-clear and Δ ≤ −threshold; non-inferior = CI upper end < threshold.
- **BEST** (continuity with E3): VtT against the best of R0 / R2 / R3 per condition, chosen on these data in the baselines' favour.

## 5. Decision (fixed now)
**The VtT method claim holds for a model if all three hold:**
1. VtT is SAFE;
2. HELP holds on at least one agreeing-error condition (`shared_heading_bias`, `c6_shared_rot`, `drift`) or real AI-depth condition;
3. on ETH3D, VtT is better than every fixed deployable remedy on pooled rotation and non-inferior to each on pooled shape.

**Wider claims and fallback:**
- "Works across models": the claim holds for both MapAnything and Pi3X.
- Otherwise the report states exactly which part failed.
- BEST is reported; it is not part of the claim, because "best per condition" requires knowing the error type.

**Descriptive:**
- per-condition table of every remedy;
- the `vtt_gate` ablation;
- runtime;
- the decision log (`vtt_decisions.jsonl`: w, edge right-probabilities, chosen explanation, σ_h, σ_c, depth scales);
- photo support vs changes on correct hints.
